# Worked Prompt Examples

These are fictional demonstrations of how to fill in the prompts. They are not claims about real company performance.

## Example 1 — B2B ERP LinkedIn post

**Prompt used:** SOC-04 — Social Caption Variations

**Filled-in brief**
- Brand: Northstar ERP (fictional)
- Product: ERP software for mid-sized apparel manufacturers
- Audience: factory owners, finance leaders, and operations managers in Bangladesh
- Platform: LinkedIn
- Goal: invite qualified prospects to a product demonstration
- Verified facts supplied: the product includes inventory, production, purchasing, and finance modules
- Brand voice: practical, credible, concise
- CTA: request a demo

**Illustrative output**
> Disconnected production, inventory, and finance data can make it harder to see what is happening across a factory.
>
> Northstar ERP brings production, inventory, purchasing, and finance workflows into one system, helping teams work from more consistent operational information.
>
> Exploring ERP for your apparel business? Request a demo to discuss your requirements.

**Human review checklist**
- Confirm the listed modules match the actual product.
- Avoid claiming guaranteed cost savings or error reduction without evidence.
- Add an approved link and confirm brand voice before publishing.
- Check that the post does not expose confidential client details.

## Example 2 — SEO content brief

**Prompt used:** SEO-02 — SEO Brief Builder

**Filled-in brief**
- Business: Northstar ERP (fictional)
- Topic: ERP software for apparel manufacturing
- Audience: apparel factory leadership and operations teams
- Market: Bangladesh
- Primary keyword: ERP software for apparel manufacturing
- Secondary terms: apparel ERP system, garment production planning software, inventory management for apparel factories
- Intent: commercial investigation
- Goal: generate demo enquiries

**Illustrative output outline**
- Proposed title: ERP Software for Apparel Manufacturing: Key Features to Evaluate
- H1: Choosing ERP Software for an Apparel Manufacturing Business
- H2: Common operational challenges
- H2: Core ERP capabilities to evaluate
- H2: Questions to ask during an ERP demo
- H2: Implementation and change-management considerations
- H2: Frequently asked questions
- CTA: Discuss your ERP requirements

**Human review checklist**
- Validate keyword intent and metrics in an SEO tool.
- Confirm every feature and implementation claim with the product team.
- Add original expertise and trustworthy references.
- Do not publish generic or unverified claims just to include keywords.

## Example 3 — B2B demo follow-up

**Prompt used:** B2B-07 — Demo Follow-Up Email

**Filled-in brief**
- Prospect: Example Apparel Ltd (fictional)
- Demo topics: production planning and inventory visibility
- Prospect priority: understand whether the workflow fits existing operations
- Agreed actions: vendor to send a module overview; prospect to share a sample workflow after internal approval
- Timeline: next week

**Illustrative output**
Subject: Follow-up on the ERP demonstration

Hello [Prospect Name],

Thank you for your time today. We discussed your team's needs around production planning and inventory visibility.

As agreed, I will share an overview of the relevant modules. Once your team has approved sharing a sample workflow, we can review it and identify any additional requirements.

Would [proposed date/time] work for a follow-up discussion?

Best regards,  
[Your Name]

**Human review checklist**
- Replace placeholders and confirm the next meeting time.
- Include only agreed actions.
- Attach only approved materials.
- Follow the company's approved communication and data-handling policies.
