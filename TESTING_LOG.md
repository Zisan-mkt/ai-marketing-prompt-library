## Suggested acceptance criteria
1. Follows the requested format and objective.
2. Uses only supplied facts or clearly labels assumptions.
3. Contains no invented statistics, sources, customer quotes, or product features.
4. Fits the intended audience and channel.
5. Includes actionable output rather than generic advice.
6. Passes human review for privacy, brand safety, and compliance.
